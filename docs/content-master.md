# Nucleus Advisors Content Master

This is the working content layer for the Nucleus public website and future editable CMS. It should guide page copy, navigation, SEO, lead magnets, newsletter topics, and later Supabase content records.

Source basis:

- Nucleus Profile 2026 PDF from `Nucleus_Data For Reference/Profile/`
- Existing Nucleus website/domain context
- Nucleus architecture and CMS notes in `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/content-platform.md`
- Comparable consulting firm content patterns, adapted to Nucleus rather than copied

Do not treat this as a one-page brochure. Nucleus should present as a full-spectrum consulting firm with strong investment banking, transaction, risk, tax, assurance, valuation, and finance operations depth. The website should become a knowledge-led acquisition engine: useful enough for serious founders, CFOs, promoters, investors, and management teams to return to it, and structured enough for the firm to capture, qualify, and nurture leads across service lines.

## Brand Positioning

Primary positioning:

> Nucleus Advisors is a full-spectrum consulting firm supporting businesses from incorporation to fundraising, control-building, compliance, transactions, expansion, and listing readiness.

Short version:

> Advisory across the full business lifecycle: incorporation to listing.

Expanded positioning:

> We work with businesses at every stage of maturity: newly incorporated companies building their first finance and compliance foundations, funded startups preparing for due diligence, growing companies strengthening controls and reporting, mature enterprises navigating tax, audit, and regulatory complexity, and market leaders evaluating transactions, restructuring, or listing-readiness.

Tone:

- senior, sharp, founder-friendly, and execution-oriented;
- closer to a modern consulting and financial advisory firm than a traditional CA practice;
- credible enough for boardrooms, practical enough for founders and operators;
- commercially ambitious without sounding opportunistic;
- educational and useful before it becomes sales-led;
- avoid generic words like "one-stop solution" unless tied to a specific outcome.

Language to use:

- "from incorporation to listing readiness"
- "decision support"
- "transaction readiness"
- "control discipline"
- "compliance confidence"
- "finance operations that scale"
- "board-ready reporting"
- "investor-ready documentation"

Language to avoid:

- "cheap services"
- "guaranteed funding"
- "assured refund"
- "best CA firm" unless supported by a verifiable ranking/source
- "we do everything" without structure
- fake urgency, fake scarcity, or exaggerated claims

Proof points from profile:

- 8 partners
- 90+ team members
- 100+ years of combined experience
- 130+ clients
- 50+ deals
- 5 offices: Gurugram, Bangalore, Jaipur, Faridabad, Bhatinda
- ranked among top M&A firms in India in FY 2022 and FY 2024
- team experience across Big 4 firms, banks, funds, and reputed consulting firms

## Service Content Engines

Each service line should function like its own mini knowledge hub, not only a static sales page. The goal is to answer most early buyer questions before a sales call, rank for long-tail SEO searches, and convert serious visitors through useful downloads and newsletter subscription.

Content rule:

- Case studies can be real only if approved for publication.
- Until approval exists, use anonymised engagement stories by sector, stage, and problem type.
- Sample documents should be educational previews, not confidential client material.
- Reports and checklists should be gated where appropriate, with clear consent to subscribe.
- Articles should internally link to services, tools, lead magnets, and related FAQs.

## SEO and Knowledge Powerhouse Strategy

The website should be built as a structured content repository, not a brochure. Each service line should accumulate pages, FAQs, guides, case studies, tools, and newsletter issues over time.

SEO principles:

- every service page targets a primary commercial keyword and related long-tail questions;
- every article links back to a service page, lead magnet, and relevant FAQ;
- every daily/live update links to a primary service page and official source;
- live updates should be periodically consolidated into evergreen explainers or FAQs;
- every lead magnet gets its own landing page;
- every tool gets an indexable explanation page plus a usable tool interface;
- every case study links to the relevant services and industries;
- every service page includes FAQ schema-ready content;
- every author/person page links to the services and articles they are associated with;
- careers pages should target CA articleship, CA, MBA, graduate, and service-line hiring searches;
- alumni and life-at-Nucleus pages should support employer-brand searches;
- avoid copied competitor phrasing;
- update high-value pages regularly with current rules, dates, and examples.

Content clusters:

- Investment Banking cluster: fundraising, investor readiness, financial modelling, due diligence, valuation, pitch deck, term sheet.
- M&A cluster: buy-side, sell-side, restructuring, merger, demerger, transaction due diligence, information memorandum.
- Risk cluster: internal audit, IFC, ICFR, process audit, controls, revenue audit, concurrent audit.
- Tax cluster: GST, direct tax, transfer pricing, international tax, compliance calendar, notices, refunds.
- Assurance cluster: audit readiness, statutory audit, bank audit, Ind AS, limited review, financial reporting.
- Valuations cluster: business valuation, ESOP, FDI, brand valuation, fair value, transaction valuation.
- Finance outsourcing cluster: accounting, MIS, payroll, vCFO, controllership, compliance operations.
- Corporate secretarial cluster: incorporation, ROC, annual filing, statutory registers, AIF compliance, board governance.
- AIF and fund management cluster: AIF setup, Angel Fund, investment manager, investor onboarding, KYC, drawdowns, unit certificates, fund compliance, portfolio reporting.
- Careers cluster: CA articleship, CA jobs, MBA finance roles, graduate analyst roles, life at Nucleus, alumni outcomes, consulting career guides.

Recommended publishing cadence:

- daily source-monitoring run for official updates and service topics;
- publish only meaningful daily/live updates after review;
- 2 service-line articles per week;
- 1 career/employer-brand post per month;
- 1 newsletter issue per week;
- 1 downloadable guide or template per month;
- 1 public tool or calculator per quarter;
- 1 case study/anonymised engagement story per month;
- 1 alumni/culture story per month, if approved content is available;
- quarterly refresh of high-value tax/regulatory pages.

Editorial standards:

- explain practical implications, not only law or theory;
- include who it applies to, documents needed, timeline, risks, and next steps;
- use tables, checklists, FAQs, and examples;
- keep calls-to-action relevant to the reader's current problem;
- do not give final legal/tax advice without professional review;
- every AI-assisted draft must have source notes and human approval.

## M&A Partner Deal Room

Phase: 3

Purpose:

> Give the M&A team a controlled, confidential portal where approved external partners, investors, acquirers, family offices, and strategic contacts can log in to view permitted deal opportunities.

Primary users:

- Nucleus M&A team;
- internal deal owners;
- external partners;
- investors and family offices;
- strategic acquirers;
- approved advisors/intermediaries.

Core capabilities:

- internal team can create deal opportunities;
- deals can be saved as draft, internal review, approved, live, paused, closed, archived;
- external partners see only deals they are permitted to access;
- teaser can be visible before NDA where appropriate;
- CIM, financials, data room files, and sensitive information require NDA/approval;
- partners can express interest, ask questions, request documents, or mark not interested;
- Nucleus team can track views, downloads, interest, Q&A, and follow-up status;
- all access and document activity is logged.

Deal fields:

- deal name/internal code;
- public teaser title;
- sector;
- geography;
- transaction type;
- ticket size/range;
- revenue/EBITDA range, if approved for sharing;
- stage;
- short teaser;
- investment/acquisition thesis;
- document access level;
- NDA required;
- deal owner;
- allowed partner groups;
- status;
- published date;
- closing/expiry date.

External partner fields:

- name;
- organisation;
- email;
- phone;
- partner type;
- sectors of interest;
- ticket size preference;
- geography preference;
- NDA status;
- access level;
- assigned relationship owner;
- status;
- activity history.

Access rules:

- no public indexing;
- login required;
- role-based and deal-level permissions;
- group-based access for partner categories;
- signed NDA or explicit approval before sensitive documents;
- every document view/download logged;
- external users cannot see internal notes;
- revoked partners lose access immediately;
- archived deals should disappear from partner view unless explicitly retained.

Suggested tables:

- `deal_opportunities`
- `deal_documents`
- `deal_access_groups`
- `deal_access_grants`
- `deal_partner_profiles`
- `deal_partner_interests`
- `deal_questions`
- `deal_answers`
- `deal_ndas`
- `deal_activity_logs`
- `deal_followups`
- `deal_status_history`

Compliance and confidentiality:

- keep deal room separate from public website content;
- no real deal should be published without internal approval;
- use anonymised teasers until confidentiality permits disclosure;
- watermark sensitive downloads where practical;
- audit logs should be immutable;
- partner access should expire or be reviewed periodically.
