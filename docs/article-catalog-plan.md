## Drafting protocol

For each article:
- 1,500-2,000 words.
- Markdown body with `##` and `###` headings, occasional `**bold**`, pull-quotes (`> `), and callouts (`:::note`, `:::insight`, `:::watch`).
- Concrete numbers; named situations; founder/operator voice.
- No "delve", "robust", "nuanced", "crucial", "multifaceted".
- 1-2 `references` per article where applicable (SEBI/RBI/MCA circulars, Companies Act sections, GST notifications — real and current).
- Excerpt: 1-2 sentence card hook.
- `reviewerStatus: 'pending'` — never approved without partner sign-off.
